# Support

Burnish is made by one person, in their spare time, and is free. There's no support team and no
promised reply times. Here's how to get the most out of it anyway.

## It fixes itself first

Burnish updates itself about once a day, so most fixes arrive without anyone asking. If something
looks wrong, **Burnish → Check for Burnish Updates…** is the quickest first step.

## An app Burnish can't check

Open the app in Burnish and look at the reason under *Status*. If you know where the app announces
its updates (a Sparkle appcast address or a GitHub repository), use **Add Update Feed…** and Burnish
watches it for you from then on. **Share This Feed…** then offers it to everyone through the
[community feeds](overrides/README.md), which are merged in batches.

## A wrong match

**More → Wrong Match? → Choose a Different Homebrew App…** fixes it on your Mac straight away.
**Report a Wrong Match on GitHub…** opens a ready-made issue so it can be fixed for everyone, in
the same batches.

## A bug

1. In **Settings → Privacy**, click **Save Diagnostics…**. The file holds your app list, the last
   check, each app's history and Burnish's own recent log lines. Nothing is sent anywhere; you
   choose whether to attach it.
2. [Open an issue](https://github.com/EnigmaticZee/Burnish/issues/new) that says what you expected,
   what happened, and attach the diagnostics file (read it first if you'd rather not share an app's
   name).

Issues are read in batches and closed automatically after two months without activity, so a report
with the diagnostics attached has the best chance of being fixed without a back-and-forth.

## Something that looks like a security problem

Open an issue with the title "Security" and no details, and a way to reach you. You'll be contacted
privately.
